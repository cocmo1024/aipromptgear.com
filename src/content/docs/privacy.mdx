This page explains how AI Prompt Gear may collect, use, and protect limited information associated with normal site operation.

AI Prompt Gear is primarily a public editorial site. It does not require reader accounts to browse reference pages, and it does not ask readers to submit sensitive personal information through the site. If future features change that posture, this notice should be updated before those features become active.

## Information that may be collected

Depending on how the site is operated, the following categories of information may be processed:

- Standard request data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and referral source.
- Basic operational logs used for uptime, performance, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.
- Search or analytics events collected in aggregated or anonymized form.
- Information voluntarily submitted through email or direct contact.

## Information readers should avoid sending

Do not send passwords, API keys, private customer data, confidential prompts, unreleased product plans, legal documents, regulated personal information, or security-sensitive details through ordinary email unless a separate agreement and secure process exists. If a correction or briefing requires sensitive context, first send a high-level description and ask whether a safer review path is appropriate.

## How information may be used

Collected information may be used to:

- Maintain site reliability and security.
- Understand which pages and topics are most useful to readers.
- Improve navigation, search, content structure, and editorial planning.
- Review business, partnership, or correction requests submitted through email.

## Third-party services

The site may rely on infrastructure, analytics, search, hosting, or future advertising providers. Those providers may process limited technical data according to their own terms and privacy practices. When a page links to an external product, documentation page, vendor site, social platform, repository, or source example, the reader is leaving AI Prompt Gear and should review that destination's privacy practices separately.

## Cookies, analytics, and future advertising

The site may use cookies, analytics scripts, or similar technologies to understand performance and usage patterns. If advertising, sponsorship technology, or affiliate tools are introduced later, this notice may be updated to reflect those systems and any relevant third-party data handling.

## Email and direct contact

If you contact the site directly, the information you provide may be retained as needed to respond, review corrections, manage business communication, or maintain an editorial record of the exchange.

## External links

Pages on this site may link to external websites, vendor resources, product pages, or other third-party materials. Those destinations operate under their own privacy practices and are not controlled by this site.

## Data retention

Operational logs, analytics information, and correspondence may be retained only as long as reasonably useful for editorial planning, security, business communication, or legal compliance.

## Policy updates

This notice may be revised as the site grows, especially if analytics, advertising, affiliate programs, or reader accounts are introduced later.
