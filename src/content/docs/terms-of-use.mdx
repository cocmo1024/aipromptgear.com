By using this site, you agree to use it for lawful, informational, and non-abusive purposes.

AI Prompt Gear is an editorial reference site. It publishes guidance, checklists, comparisons, prompt structures, and workflow analysis for readers who need to think more clearly about AI systems. It does not create a client, advisory, employment, vendor, or professional-services relationship with readers.

## Content use

Unless otherwise stated, the site's original editorial content is provided for informational use only. You may quote brief portions with proper attribution, but you should not reproduce the site in bulk or present its content as your own.

Do not scrape, mirror, repackage, or bulk republish the site in a way that substitutes for visiting the original pages. Do not remove attribution, source context, policy links, or disclosure language from excerpts. Prompt templates and checklists may be adapted for internal work, but readers remain responsible for reviewing the output, rights, privacy implications, and suitability of any adapted material.

| Use case | Allowed expectation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal discussion | Link to pages or quote short excerpts with attribution. | Do not copy the site into a private library without permission. |
| Prompt adaptation | Use templates as a starting point for your own workflow. | Do not republish templates as original work without meaningful adaptation. |
| Training or workshops | Reference the site as source material. | Do not sell copied pages as a standalone course or dataset. |
| Automated crawling | Respect robots, rate limits, and normal access behavior. | Do not scrape the site for bulk republication. |
| Commercial decisions | Use pages to structure questions and reviews. | Verify current facts, contracts, and policies independently. |

## Acceptable use

You agree not to use the site to:

- interfere with normal operation, availability, or security;
- misrepresent the site's content as official guidance from a vendor or regulator;
- use automated access in a way that overloads the site;
- submit unlawful, abusive, misleading, or confidential material through contact channels;
- reproduce large portions of the site for a competing content product without permission.

## Limitation of responsibility

AI Prompt Gear is not responsible for decisions readers make after using the site, including tool purchases, model routing choices, generated outputs, workflow changes, compliance interpretations, security controls, or vendor contracts. Use the site as a reference input, not as the final approval authority for high-stakes decisions.

## High-risk use

Do not treat a checklist, prompt, comparison, or workflow on this site as approval to bypass your own review process. Teams using these materials for production AI systems should keep separate records for vendor terms, security sign-off, privacy review, model behavior testing, customer impact, and rollback readiness.

| Situation | Reader responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Production deployment | Test behavior in your own environment before relying on any prompt, agent pattern, or evaluation checklist. |
| Procurement or budgeting | Validate current pricing, contractual limits, data handling terms, and support commitments with the vendor. |
| Regulated or sensitive workflows | Obtain qualified legal, compliance, security, or operational review before acting on site material. |
| Public reuse | Attribute the site where appropriate and avoid implying that AI Prompt Gear has reviewed your derivative work. |
